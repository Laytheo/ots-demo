The Ontological Type System is a method for personal knowledge management built on classical realism. Where most note systems organize by topic or task, OTS organizes by what kind of thing each note's referent actually is: a [[Person]], [[Place]], [[Work]], [[Event]], [[Idea]], or [[Journal]] entry. There are six types, and only six. Everything fits. The result is a [graph](/graph) that grows by accretion rather than by hierarchy, and a vault that can hold a lifetime of thinking without ever needing to be reorganized.
