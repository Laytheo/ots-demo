A podcast about the strange corners of history and the world: unexplained events, odd crimes, folklore, and the people who chased the answers. Jimmy Akin and Dom Bettinelli take one mystery per episode and walk through what is known, what is claimed, and which explanation holds up best.

It is what I put on for chores and drives. Most episodes leave me with one detail I keep turning over for a week.
