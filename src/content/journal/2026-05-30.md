Saturday got away from me in a good way. I ran into my neighbor, [[Desmond Philips|Desmond]], while taking out the trash, and we struck up a conversation in his driveway that ran most of an hour. He explained how he used to find property corners from old deeds.

- [[Jimmy Akin's Mysterious World]] / [[The Kentucky Meat Shower]]
  - Had it on while I did the dishes. In 1876 meat fell from a clear sky over a strip of Bath County, Kentucky, and two men tasted it and guessed lamb or venison. The best explanation is a flock of startled vultures, which is somehow worse than a miracle.
  - What stays with me is the tasting. Something impossible lands in your yard and a man's first instinct is to eat it. We are curious creatures.
