[[Place]]: a location or institution. Both physical places ([[Locust Ridge Preserve]], [[Hudson Flea Market]]) and organizational entities ([[University of Notre Dame|Notre Dame]], [[Grace Reformed Church]]). Institutions have a kind of locatedness; people gather in them, are formed by them, leave them.
